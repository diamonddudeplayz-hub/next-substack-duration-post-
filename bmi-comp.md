# Badger Meter (BMI) — comp & valuation anchor for the ITRI thesis

**Purpose:** BMI is the *finished* version of the ITRI trade — a smart-meter company the market has already re-rated into a "hardware-enabled software platform." It quantifies ITRI's destination and corroborates the lumpiness caveat. The arbitrage lives in ITRI (pre-framework, 13x); BMI prices where that framework gets you (32x).

## BMI Investor Day (May 21, 2026) — the ITRI playbook, verbatim

BMI's *first* Investor Day in 121 years. Framing (Noverini): "the evolution… from a **120-year-old meter manufacturer to a hardware-enabled software platform**." This is ITRI's own pending reframe, run by a company already paid for it.

**The financial framework that earns the 32x multiple** (what ITRI's Investor Day must supply its own version of):
- High-single-digit **6–8% organic** CAGR, 5-yr plan (won't be 6–8% every year — "could be 13, could be 4"; '26 is an outlier/air-pocket year)
- Double-digit Beyond-The-Meter growth; double-digit software growth
- SaaS **$75M today → $150M by 2030**
- Gross margin normalized **39–42%**; incremental margins **25–30%** (reframed from EBIT → **EBITDA**)
- FCF conversion **>100%** of net income; TAM reframed **$5B → $15B** (water-cycle expansion)
- Last 5 yrs: 17% revenue CAGR, 28% software CAGR, +470bps margin; 2025 first >$900M rev, 24% EBITDA, 120% FCF conversion, 3-yr avg ROIC 33%

**Software architecture presented:** BlueEdge (platform brand, 4 pillars: sensing / connectivity / insights-action / collaboration-support) · BEACON (MDM) · EyeOnWater (consumer app) · field app (iOS/Android) · **Cobalt** (self-developed GenAI, built in, not third-party) · **OneNetwork** (unifies all Beyond-The-Meter sensors — formally launched Q3 at WEFTEC) · SmartCover + UDlive (sewer-line monitoring, global leader) · s::can/ATi/Syrinix/Telog (water quality, pressure).

## Five transferable frames for the ITRI post

1. **"Hardware-enabled software, not pure-play" = AI-disruption defense.** Stuyvenberg: software "attached to every device we sell… converts the installed base into a **compounding annuity**," physical moat "defends against pure-play AI software competitors." ITRI's Resiliency/Outcomes = same structure. Rebuts the bear case pressuring both names.
2. **Installed base = compounding annuity, 100% attachment.** BMI's spring is wound (100% attach). ITRI's is coiled (17.5M DI endpoints, ~3.8M running apps). Same mechanism; ITRI hasn't activated it — that's upside not in either multiple.
3. **Lumpiness, second-source corroboration.** Wrocklage's "project pacing and order rate unevenness": literally "project pacing **air pocket**," AMI deployments compared to **ERP implementations** (once-in-a-career, 1–5 yr). Independent validation of "deployment lumpiness ≠ demand deterioration."
4. **Funding not stimulus-dependent.** "Very little, if any, [IIJA/ARPA] has gone to metering"; driven by asset life, nonrevenue water, labor efficiency; funded by rate base, muni/revenue bonds, WIFIA, enterprise funds. Parallels ITRI's "distribution CapEx not crowded out."
5. **"Why now" for a first Investor Day.** Ken: acquisitions + internal investment "fueled this evolution… the right time to reinforce certain messages." ITRI's exact setup (integrate Resiliency → drip products → synthesize).

## Nuance / risk to calibrate

- **BMI refused segmentation** ("no plans to start doing segmentation") — yet gets 32x on a clean *consolidated* algorithm. Read-through: ITRI may not need granular segment ARR to re-rate; a credible consolidated framework + a dated Investor Day could suffice. If ITRI *does* give segment-level ARR targets, it out-discloses a 32x company (bull case).
- **BMI is the done trade** (expensive, ~3% to target, '26 organic negative at 32x) — a bear would call BMI overvalued rather than ITRI cheap. Fair framing for the post: a 2x+ multiple gap for a comparable transition is far wider than the quality gap justifies; ITRI's Investor Day is the catalyst that closes part of it. BMI is not itself a long here — no measurement-lag edge.
