# Badger Meter (BMI) — comp & valuation anchor for the ITRI thesis

**Purpose:** BMI is the *finished* version of the ITRI trade — a smart-meter company the market has already re-rated into a "hardware-enabled software platform." It quantifies ITRI's destination and corroborates the lumpiness caveat. The arbitrage lives in ITRI (pre-framework, 13x); BMI prices where that framework gets you (32x).

## Five transferable frames for the ITRI post

1. **"Hardware-enabled software, not pure-play" = AI-disruption defense.** Stuyvenberg: software "attached to every device we sell… converts the installed base into a **compounding annuity**," physical moat "defends against pure-play AI software competitors." ITRI's Resiliency/Outcomes = same structure. Rebuts the bear case pressuring both names.
2. **Installed base = compounding annuity, 100% attachment.** BMI's spring is wound (100% attach). ITRI's is coiled (17.5M DI endpoints, ~3.8M running apps). Same mechanism; ITRI hasn't activated it — that's upside not in either multiple.
3. **Lumpiness, second-source corroboration.** Wrocklage's "project pacing and order rate unevenness": literally "project pacing **air pocket**," AMI deployments compared to **ERP implementations** (once-in-a-career, 1–5 yr). Independent validation of "deployment lumpiness ≠ demand deterioration."
4. **Funding not stimulus-dependent.** "Very little, if any, [IIJA/ARPA] has gone to metering"; driven by asset life, nonrevenue water, labor efficiency; funded by rate base, muni/revenue bonds, WIFIA, enterprise funds. Parallels ITRI's "distribution CapEx not crowded out."
5. **"Why now" for a first Investor Day.** Ken: acquisitions + internal investment "fueled this evolution… the right time to reinforce certain messages." ITRI's exact setup (integrate Resiliency → drip products → synthesize).

## Nuance / risk to calibrate

- **BMI refused segmentation** ("no plans to start doing segmentation") — yet gets 32x on a clean *consolidated* algorithm. Read-through: ITRI may not need granular segment ARR to re-rate; a credible consolidated framework + a dated Investor Day could suffice. If ITRI *does* give segment-level ARR targets, it out-discloses a 32x company (bull case).
- **BMI is the done trade** (expensive, ~3% to target, '26 organic negative at 32x) — a bear would call BMI overvalued rather than ITRI cheap. Fair framing for the post: a 2x+ multiple gap for a comparable transition is far wider than the quality gap justifies; ITRI's Investor Day is the catalyst that closes part of it. BMI is not itself a long here — no measurement-lag edge.

## Why BMI's multiple > ITRI's — historically (TIKR, NTM P/E)

| | Q1'24 | Q2'25 | Q4'25 | Q2'26 | Q3'26 | Now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NTM P/E | 45.8x | **49.2x** (peak) | 33.1x | 30.9x | **25.9x** (trough) | 32.1x |
| Price | $162 | $245 | $174 | $148 | $124 | $156 |

**The premium is shrinking, not fixed.** BMI de-rated ~45% off its 2025 peak (stock $245→$124, P/E 49x→26x) — the "water SaaS premium" is conditional, not granted.

**It compressed because growth rolled over.** Last 3-yr EPS CAGR **28.5%** → forward 2-yr EPS CAGR **2.0%** (rev 3.2%, EBITDA 2.8%). LTM EPS peaked $4.79 (Q3'25), flat-to-down (~$4.3) since. Market still pays 32x largely on the *memory* of 25%+ growth + reputation.

**It's a certainty premium, not a growth premium:**
- Earned part — 34-yr Dividend Aristocrat, >100% FCF conversion, ~33% 3-yr ROIC, 0.64 beta, 100% software attach, pure-play water, light B/S (2.1x), and a *disclosed framework* the Street can model.
- ITRI = mirror-image profile the market discounts: lumpy regulatory-calendar revenue, 2.3x leverage, ARR 3 quarters old w/ no segment framework, FY modeled flat-to-down.

**Punchline:** BMI = 32x for ~2% fwd EPS growth; ITRI = ~13x with ARR +21%. The gap is an **uncertainty-discount** gap, not a growth gap. Cure for ITRI: shrink the discount (framework + sustained ARR), not grow faster. **Caution:** BMI proves the premium is *rented* — 49x→26x the moment growth disappointed. ITRI's re-rate is earned quarter-by-quarter by the ARR print holding.

## Key twist: BMI does NOT disclose formal ARR — ITRI out-discloses it

Per the Q3 call + May Investor Day transcripts, BMI reports **no formal ARR metric**. It discloses: "SaaS revenue" ~$75M today / $150M-by-2030 target, 100% BEACON attach rate, ~28% 5-yr software CAGR (backward-looking) — and Ken explicitly **refused segmentation** ("no plans to start doing segmentation"). No ARR KPI, no net retention, no segment recurring split, no quarterly recurring growth rate.

So ITRI (formal ARR $417M +21%, a primary 10-K metric) **out-discloses a company trading at 2.4x its multiple.** ⇒ The 32x-vs-13x gap is NOT a recurring-disclosure-granularity gap. BMI gets paid for a **legible consolidated algorithm** (6–8% organic, 39–42% GM, >100% FCF conversion, double-digit software) + predictability (0.64 beta) + a 34-yr track record — modelable end-to-end *without* an ARR line.

**Implication for the thesis (sharper version):** the ARR line was never the catalyst — ITRI introduced ARR 3 quarters ago and didn't re-rate; BMI gets the premium with no ARR at all. What closes the gap is **trust that a forward framework is durable and modelable**, not the metric itself. The Investor Day matters because it's management staking credibility to a multi-year framework (BMI-style), + a few quarters of ARR *holding* — not because it hands analysts an ARR SOTP to plug in.
